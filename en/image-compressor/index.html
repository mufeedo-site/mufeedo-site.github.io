Image Compressor

Pick images, set the quality and download smaller files. All on your device.

    How to use Image Compressor

    1. Choose one or more images from your device.
    2. Move the quality slider. 70 to 80 often saves a lot with no visible change.
    3. If the image is very large, set a max width such as 1600 pixels.
    4. Download each image after compression.

    Good to know

    Compression re-encodes the image at slightly lower quality, which cuts camera photos a lot.

    WebP gives a smaller file than JPG at similar quality and works in all modern browsers.

    Transparent images get a white background when saved as JPG.

    Images are processed on your device only, so this is fine for document and ID photos.

    FAQ

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser on your device.

    Does quality drop?

    A little, depending on your setting. Try 75 and compare.

    Why is the file sometimes bigger?

    If the image is already heavily compressed, compressing again can make it larger. Keep the original.
