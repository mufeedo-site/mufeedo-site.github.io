Merge PDF Files

Pick PDF files, put them in order and download one file.

    How to use Merge PDF Files

    1. Choose two or more PDF files.
    2. Put them in order with the arrows.
    3. Press Merge files and download the result.

    Good to know

    The output has every page of the files in the order you chose.

    Password protected files cannot be merged. Remove the protection first.

    Merging happens on your device, so it suits personal and sensitive documents.

    FAQ

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser on your device.

    Why was a file rejected?

    Usually it is password protected or damaged.

    Does it change page quality?

    No. Pages are copied as they are.
