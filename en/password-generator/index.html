Password Generator

A random password made on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

How to use Password Generator

  1. Move the slider to choose a length. 16 or more is a good default.
  2. Pick the character types.
  3. Press Copy and save it in a password manager.

Good to know

The password uses the browser secure random generator (crypto.getRandomValues), not an ordinary random function.

Length matters most. 16 characters with mixed types gives a strong result.

Use a different password for every account and turn on two-step verification where you can.

We left out look-alike characters such as O, l, 0 and 1 so the password is easy to read.

FAQ

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser on your device.

What length is best?

At least 16 characters for important accounts.

Do I need symbols?

Not if the password is long, but symbols add strength.
